Romania's retail sales volumes grew 0.9% in August compared with the same month last year, according to data from the National Institute of Statistics (INS) published on 6 October. On a working-day-adjusted basis the increase was 0.1%. It is the first year-on-year rise after twelve consecutive monthly declines: in July, sales were still 6.1% lower than a year earlier.

## Where the growth came from

The recovery is uneven across categories. Compared with August last year:

- Non-food sales rose 2.7%
- Fuel sales rose 2.5%
- Food, beverages and tobacco fell 2.3%

Against July, total retail sales were up 2.3%.

## Why it matters for brands

One month of growth is not yet a recovery, and the split matters more than the headline. Food volumes are still shrinking, so grocery brands are competing for a smaller basket, and food buyers are likely to stay cautious about new listings and demanding on promotional support. Non-food categories such as household and personal care are where volume is returning first, which makes them the easier conversation with a chain buyer this autumn.
