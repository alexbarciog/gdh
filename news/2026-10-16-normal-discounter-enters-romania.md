NORMAL, a Danish discount chain selling personal care, beauty and household products, has opened its first two Romanian stores. The first opened on 30 September at Mega Mall in Bucharest, with 311 sq m of space, and the second on 2 October at Shopping City Timișoara, with 445 sq m, according to the landlord, NEPI Rockcastle. Romania is NORMAL's 14th European market and its first in Central and Eastern Europe.

## What NORMAL sells

The stores carry around 3,500 products from well-known brands, covering beauty (including Korean and Japanese beauty), body and hair care, make-up, Asian favourites and everyday household essentials. The range changes often, with new products arriving constantly. The company says it runs more than 1,000 stores across Europe, [Business24 reported](https://business24.ro/retail-discount/extindere-retailer-normal-magazine-bucuresti-timisoara-1669308), naming dm, BIPA and Action among the chains it will compete with in Romania. Chief executive Jakob Frølich Maarbjerg described Romania as an attractive and growing market.

## Discount formats keep spreading

The arrival comes as other discount and specialist formats keep expanding. PENNY opened stores in Șura Mică (Sibiu County) and Frasin (Suceava County), bringing its Romanian network to 474 units, [retail-FMCG.ro reported](https://www.retail-fmcg.ro/analize/retail-fmcg-sinteza-2-octombrie-2026.html). In its latest retail report, Colliers describes a maturing market in which investment is shifting from new developments towards modernising existing ones.

## Why it matters for brands

A new non-food discounter is a new buyer for exactly the categories drugstores and supermarket non-food aisles also sell. NORMAL's model of a fast-rotating, brand-led assortment can give a personal care brand national visibility quickly, but discount listings come with their own terms: tight price points, specific pack formats and little tolerance for slow lines. A chain in its first months in a market is often still building its supplier list, so brands in beauty and household goods should watch where it opens next.
