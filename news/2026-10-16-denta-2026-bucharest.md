DENTA 2026 opens today at the ROMEXPO Exhibition Centre in Bucharest and runs until Sunday, 18 October. Dental Tribune describes it as the largest dental exhibition in Romania. This year the organiser, Romexpo, is running it in Pavilion B2 together with ROMMEDICA, its general medicine fair, and a new National Salon of Optics and Optometry (SNOO), so visitors can see dentistry, medicine and optics in one visit.

## What is on show

The DENTA section covers equipment for clinics and laboratories, dental units and instruments, materials, implantology, orthodontics, radiology and imaging, and CAD/CAM and digital workflows, according to Romexpo. It is aimed at dentists, dental technicians, clinic managers and distributors. The fair is open from 10:00 to 18:00 on Friday and Saturday, and from 10:00 to 16:00 on Sunday, [Jurnalul de Afaceri reported](https://jurnaluldeafaceri.ro/stiri/denta-rommedica-snoo-2026-aduce-in-prim-plan-la-romexpo-cele-mai-recente-tehnologii/).

## The scientific programme

The Romanian Medical Association (AMR) holds its congress alongside the fair. Its in-person AMR Forum runs on 16 and 17 October in the Hygea Hall of Pavilion B2, with sessions on oral and systemic health, digitally guided implantology, orthodontics at different ages, dental veneers and the use of AI in managing a dental practice.

Elsewhere in Europe this week, the Digital Dentistry Society's conference in Antwerp is expected to draw up to 400 professionals on 16 and 17 October, focusing on AI-based imaging, clinical 3D printing and digital orthodontics, [Conference and Meetings World reported](https://www.c-mw.net/antwerp-welcomes-digital-dentistry-societys-2026-conference/).

## Why it matters for brands

Dentistry buys on trust. A practitioner who has used the same material for years rarely switches because of a brochure, which makes a trade fair one of the few moments when practices and laboratories try new products hands-on. For a dental brand new to Romania, the fair introduces the product. What turns a trial into repeat orders is what happens afterwards: someone calling on the practice every week, with the product in stock.
