DENTA 2026 opens today at the ROMEXPO Exhibition Centre in Bucharest and runs until 18 October. Dental Tribune describes it as the largest dental exhibition in Romania.

Organised by Romexpo, the fair brings together clinical equipment, digital dentistry solutions, oral hygiene products, pharmaceutical developments and materials for dental medicine and laboratory practice. It is aimed at dentists, dental technicians and professional buyers, and is used for business meetings as well as product presentations. The programme and exhibitor list are published by the organiser at denta.ro.

## Why it matters for brands

Dentistry buys on trust. A practitioner who has used the same material for years rarely switches because of a brochure, which makes a trade fair one of the few moments when practices and laboratories try new products hands-on. For a dental brand new to Romania, the fair introduces the product. What turns a trial into repeat orders is what happens afterwards: someone calling on the practice every week, with the product in stock.
