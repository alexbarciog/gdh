GDH — Global Distribution Holdings has launched a new website at gdh-group.com. It replaces the previous site and is written for the people we work with every day: the brands whose products we distribute, the chain buyers we supply, and the dental practices, clinics and laboratories we deliver to.

## What is on the new site

The site is organised around the two channels we serve. **Retail** covers national grocery, drugstore, convenience and cash-and-carry chains, plus private label. **Dental** covers practices, clinics and laboratories. Each sector has its own page explaining how that channel buys and how we work within it.

Our services each have a page of their own as well:

- Chain listings — preparing the submission, taking the buyer meeting and buying the opening order
- Stock and delivery — one delivery into our bay, then our stock on our routes
- Shelf execution — field teams holding the planogram in store
- Sell-out visibility — weekly rate of sale per chain and per SKU
- Returns and recalls — collected on the delivery round and traced by batch

## Sending us your range

Brands can now send their range directly from the [Contact sales](/contact-sales) page: product list, monthly capacity and the channel they want to reach. A first reply usually arrives within two working days, and it costs the brand nothing — GDH never invoices the brands it carries.

## News, as it happens

This news page is where we will publish GDH's own announcements from now on: changes to the network, new channels and capability, and notes on the categories we distribute. Every item is dated and also available in our [RSS feed](/news/feed.xml).

The site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and makes no requests to any other server.
