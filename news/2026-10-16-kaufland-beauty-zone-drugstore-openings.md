Kaufland Romania opened two new stores on 8 October, in Galați and Satu Mare, and launched a pilot drugstore and beauty concept at its Barbu Văcărescu store in Bucharest. According to retail-FMCG.ro, the area, called Beauty Zone, brings in more than 600 new products.

The same day, dm drogerie markt opened a store in Bucharest's Pantelimon district, and BIPA, part of REWE Group, opened its tenth Romanian store in Roșiori de Vede, the chain's first in Teleorman county.

Also in Pantelimon, Cometex opened Pantelimon Retail Park, an investment of more than €9 million with 5,800 sq m of commercial space. Its tenants include dm, BIPA, Altex, Pepco, Sinsay, TEDi and Froo.

## Why it matters for brands

A hypermarket chain building a dedicated beauty area is a sign that health and beauty is being treated as a destination category, not an aisle filler. For personal care brands, that means more shelf space in play, and a range review in which the buyer decides which of 600-plus lines earn a permanent place. Retail parks that put dm, BIPA and discounters side by side also mean drugstore and discount formats now compete for the same shopper in the same car park.
