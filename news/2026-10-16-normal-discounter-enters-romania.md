NORMAL, a Danish variety-discount chain built around non-food FMCG products, has entered Romania with its first two stores, at Mega Mall in Bucharest and Shopping City Timișoara. Both shopping centres are owned by NEPI Rockcastle, and ACROSS Magazine reported the openings on 6 October.

Discount formats kept growing elsewhere too. PENNY opened stores in Șura Mică (Sibiu County) and Frasin (Suceava County), bringing its Romanian network to 474 units.

## Why it matters for brands

A new non-food discounter is a new buyer for exactly the categories drugstores and supermarket non-food aisles also sell. Discount listings can bring real volume, but they come with their own terms: tight price points, specific pack formats and little tolerance for slow lines. For brands in personal care and household goods, NORMAL is worth watching as it opens more stores: a chain in its first months in a market is often still building its supplier list.
