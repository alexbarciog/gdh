Kaufland Romania has launched a pilot drugstore and beauty area, called Beauty Zone, at its store on Barbu Văcărescu Street in Bucharest. The space holds about 4,000 products, more than 600 of them new to Kaufland's range, [ESM Magazine reports](https://www.esmmagazine.com/retail/kaufland-romania-introduces-beauty-zone-concept-323763).

## What the pilot includes

The area is organised by theme rather than by product type: new arrivals, trends, skincare and face masks, perfumery, Korean beauty and a dedicated make-up section. Kaufland's own drugstore brand, Bevola, has its own display, and the retailer plans to expand it by November. Branded make-up from Essence, Catrice, Avon and Rimmel joins the assortment.

The pilot also adds services that hypermarkets rarely offer: an AI mirror that analyses the shopper's complexion and suggests products, and a nail bar open Monday to Saturday from noon to 4pm. "We want customers to find everything they need in one place at Kaufland," said Valer Hancaș, Kaufland Romania's director of communications and corporate affairs. No wider roll-out has been announced.

## A busy week for drugstores

On the same day, 8 October, Kaufland opened new stores in Galați and Satu Mare, dm drogerie markt opened in Bucharest's Pantelimon district, and BIPA, part of REWE Group, opened its tenth Romanian store in Roșiori de Vede, its first in Teleorman county, [retail-FMCG.ro reported](https://www.retail-fmcg.ro/analize/retail-sinteza-9-octombrie-2026.html). Also in Pantelimon, Cometex opened a 5,800 sq m retail park, an investment of more than €9 million, with dm, BIPA, Altex, Pepco, Sinsay, TEDi and Froo among its tenants.

## Why it matters for brands

A hypermarket chain building a dedicated beauty area is a sign that health and beauty is being treated as a destination category, not an aisle filler. For personal care brands, that means more shelf space in play, but also more competition from the retailer's own label, which is getting dedicated space of its own. If the pilot is extended, the buyer will decide which of the 600-plus new lines earn a permanent place, so performance in the test store matters.
